PARAGRAPH 2 - INQUIRY
---------------------
Paragraph 2 (4-6 sentences): Reflect on the past week.
Summarize meaningful moments, values, or lessons from the activities.
Mention what made them important or fulfilling.


PARAGRAPH 2 - PERSUASION / DELIBERATION
---------------------------------------
Paragraph 2 (4-6 sentences): Transition to next week.
Tie 2-3 suggestions to the rated activities
(importance/fun 4-5 first; include one lower-rated only if helpful).
Use 'because' to connect suggestions to values.


PARAGRAPH 3 - PERSUASION / DELIBERATION
---------------------------------------
Paragraph 3 (2-3 sentences): Offer a simple next-week plan
linked to the narrative. End with an encouraging line.


PARAGRAPH 3 - INQUIRY
---------------------
Paragraph 3 (2-3 sentences): Do NOT propose a plan.
Instead, briefly reflect on what this story means for you
and close with a warm, reassuring line.
