================================================================================
CONDITION B - PERSON-TAILORED GENERATION WITHOUT EXPLICIT ARGUMENTATION GUIDANCE
================================================================================

PURPOSE
-------
Condition B uses persona/activity information and the natural-language narrative
prompt, without explicit dialogue-type labels or argumentation-scheme guidance.


STYLE PROMPT
------------
LOW creativity:

STYLE: Plain language. Start with a positive, validating line.
Factual, supportive, concise.


HIGH creativity:

STYLE: Engaging but concise. Start with a positive, validating line.
Factual and supportive. keep imagery minimal.


GENERATION PROMPT
-----------------
Name: {persona_id}

Activities:
{activities_text}

Narrative Prompt:
{topic}

Write a short narrative in second person ("you").

Tone:
- Supportive
- Factual
- Suitable for older adults

Use clear, simple language.
Avoid slang and exaggerated imagery.

Target length:
150-180 words (maximum 180 words).

Structure:

Paragraph 1:
Warm opening about the participant's activities.

Paragraph 2:
Respond naturally to the narrative prompt using the participant's activities and experiences.

Paragraph 3:
Closing reflection that reinforces purpose, meaning, or encouragement.

Output requirements:
- Exactly three paragraphs.
- No headings.
- No lists.
- No labels.


NARRATIVE PROMPTS
-----------------
Stories 1-2:
Dear Coach, please, try to convince me about what activities I should do
the coming week so that the week will become a happy week!

Stories 3-4:
What should I do/you suggest me to do, to make the coming week more
recovering than the past week?

Stories 5-6:
Dear Coach, did I do something important the past week?

Stories 7-8:
Dear Coach, tell me how I have fulfilled my motives the past week/ month.
